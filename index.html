Straight talk

What this app is,
and isn’t.

How it works

ScrollGuard is its own Android app that shows Instagram’s website in a built-in browser and hides Reels on top of it. It contains no code taken from the official Instagram app. You log in on Instagram’s own pages; ScrollGuard does not read, store or send your password.

Known limits

  • It is the web version, so some official-app features are missing or different, such as push notifications.
  • If Instagram changes its pages, a Reel may slip through until the rules are updated.
  • Logging in with Facebook may not work in an embedded browser. Use your Instagram login.
  • ScrollGuard is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.

Questions

Good to know.

Is this the official Instagram app?

No. ScrollGuard is an independent app and is not made by or connected to Instagram or Meta. It shows Instagram’s website with Reels hidden.

Is my login safe?

You sign in on Instagram’s own web pages. ScrollGuard has no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and it does not collect your password. As with any app, only install it from this site.

Will it work on iPhone?

Not right now. It is Android only.

Why can’t I see Reels in a message someone sent me?

That is on purpose. A shared Reel shows a small “hidden” notice instead of playing. The message itself is still there.

Can Instagram break it?

Sometimes. If Instagram changes how its pages are built, a Reel might show up until the rules are refreshed. Updates will fix that.
